::: facts
- **When** — Every Tuesday, 7:30 pm Central
- **Where** — Shoreline Brewery, 208 W. Wabash St, Michigan City, IN
- **Cost** — Free to play, free to watch
- **Bring** — Your instrument. Everything else is already there.
:::

=== block

^ What's coming up

## Next few Tuesdays

::: schedule
limit: 4
empty: The next dates go up here as they're set. It's every Tuesday either way.
:::

Every date is the same night: the set up and walk-in sign ups start around 6:45, the house band
opens at 7:30, and the floor is open from 8 until close to 10.
[See all dates](/events.html)

::: callout
Want a band set, or a specific song backed by the house band? Say so before the
night through [signup form here](https://forms.gle/PE3HYF6z3gBVSZFQ8),
or reach out to us at the [Open Jam Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/shorelineopenjam) —
band sets get planned around changeovers, and a song request with a key attached
is a much faster "yes".
:::

^ First time here?

## Turn up and say hello

::: numbers
1. Read [how it works](/how-it-works.html) — five minutes, and you'll know what to expect.
2. Come a little before 7:30 and find the house band during setup.
3. Play.
:::

Nothing needs arranging in advance. If you'd rather it did — a band set, or a
specific song you want backed — say so ahead of time through the
[Open Jam Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/shorelineopenjam).
