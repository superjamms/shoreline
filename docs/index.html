Open jam, every Tuesday

Bring an instrument. The backline is already on stage, the house band hosts, and you can play a set, sit in, or just get on something.

When
Every Tuesday, 7:30 pm Central
Where
Shoreline Brewery, 208 W. Wabash St, Michigan City, IN
Cost
Free to play, free to watch
Bring
Your instrument. Everything else is already there.

Want a band set, or a specific song backed by the house band? Say so before the night through signup form here, or reach out to us at the Open Jam Facebook page — band sets get planned around changeovers, and a song request with a key attached is a much faster "yes".
