Frequently Asked Questions

Question: I've never been to an open mic/jam before - how does these work?*

In short, these are usually loosely structured events that allow you, at any level of musicianship, to come play your instrument, without fear of mistakes, heckling, or ridicule.

Each jam is a bit different in how they cater to musicians, what type of music they play, and the vibe.

It is usually best to just come out to an event the first time, without your instrument. Observe, meet the coordinator, house band and the regulars. Ask questions, and see if the vibe fits you.

Question: I'm familiar with open mic/jams, but how does Shoreline Open Jam work?

The short answer is: Just bring your instrument. You'll like get a chance to play, no matter your level.

The backline (the equipmment supporting the open jam) is immense. Even if you don't bring your own instrument, it is likely you'll still be able to play (but you will definitely be more comfortable on your own).

The house band includes seasoned working musicians that also teach music. So you also have a solid backing band if you need it.

See the How it works article, it outlines in depth how the Shoreline Open Jam nights are planned, and how they work.

What types of music are played at the Shoreline Open Jam?

No hard or fast rules.

It is common for the house band to cover 60's-90's classic rock, reggae or country standards, but also common to see solo and bands covering metal, funk or classical.

I'm coming solo. Can the house band cover my song request?

The short answer is "it depends".

If you have specific song requests that would make or break your night, it is best to request early via the online sign up form or contact us on our Facebook page.

We are working up a common song list based on previous jam set lists. Once we post out, you will be able to see what we can confidently cover.

On the other hand, the house band comprises seasoned musicians. If you come prepared with a chart, that may make the difference between a hard "no", and "maybe", meaning, we'll give it a go, but it may not be what you envisioned.

If the house band cannot accomodate you, you can also bring in your own backing tracks on a smart phone or tablet - we can probably get that to work with our Mixer.

Am I required to sign up to play at the Shoreline Open Jam

The short answer is "no".

You can generally walk in to an event and be able to play.

But you may not be able to play to the exact capacity or plan you intended.

We recommend you communicate with us reasonably early before an event so we can best accomodate you as well as the other bands, soloists and walk-ins.

The most proactive way is to use the online sign-in form) or talk to us directly on our Facebook page.

The next best way is to informally sign up before the house band starts during each event (before 7:30 pm).

What gear do I need to bring?

Your instrument.

That's it.

We provide the backline - the amps, cables, mics, mixer, monitors and lighting. The venue provides the PA. We also host guitar and bass amp modelers with a host of built in effects (FX).

If you are particular about your FX pedalboard, or really want to bring an amp, that is OK too.
