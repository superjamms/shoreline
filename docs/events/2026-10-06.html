The shape of the night

What's happening

Nothing week-specific is set yet, so this is the usual run: the house band sets up the backline and takes sign-ups from about 6:45, opens with a short set at 7:30, and from 8 onwards it's guest bands, solo spots and the open jam until close to 10.

Want a band set, or a specific song backed by the house band? Say so before Tuesday through the Open Jam Facebook page. Name the song and the key and the answer comes much faster. Walk-ins are always fine too — just tell the house band you want to play.
