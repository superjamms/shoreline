# Signup Form Specification

Mirrors the live Google Form:

## Form description

Show up and bring an instrument.

Play your own set solo or with your band, ask the house band to back you, or just jam.

Need house band backing or backing tracks? Sign up early so we can prepare.

Questions?  Message us at the Shoreline Brewery Open Jam Facebook page or email <admin@superjamm.live>.

## Section 1: Signup

### Here's how we handle your info (title and description block)

- Name: used to call you up on event night. Shown online only if you choose Yes below.
- Email: optional, never posted. Only our event coordinator and house band see it.
- Songs: set lists and requests appear in event forecasts and recaps, with or without your name.
- Retention: we delete signups a couple of weeks after each event.
- Changed your mind? Email <admin@superjamm.live> and we'll anonymize anything posted.
- Photos/video: we don't take or post them by default. Share your own on our Facebook page, or give them to the event coordinator and we'll post them, but only with permission from everyone in them.

### Name (or Band Name)

Short answer, required.

> At a minimum, we need to know who you are so we can call on you during the event.
> If you are providing a band name, please clear with your bandmates first.

### Show your name (or band name) on our site and Facebook page?

- No, keep me anonymous
- Yes, show my name

### Contact email

Short answer, optional.

## Section 3: Set List Support

We need some information if you have a specific set list you need supported by the house backing band.
If you skipped the email question, use Back to add it. We can't prepare your set without a way to reach you.

While the house band includes seasoned musicians, they need to know as early as possible what you want covered so they can determine whether they can cover it, and sufficient time to prepare.

You can also message us directly on the Shoreline Brewery Open Jam Facebook page or email at <admin@superjamm.live>
