# Signup Form Specification

## Who?

- Band
- Soloist
- Jam Sit-In

## What to play?

- Songlist to play - including song, artist, key it is played - bonus points if you have charts (ultimate guitar, songbook pro, etc)
- Open to anything (for open jam)

## What instruments am I playing?

List of common instruments:

- Drums
- Bass
- Guitar
- Horns
- Keys
- Other

## Gear

The backline provides most of what you might need,
including guitar and bass amps (combo of amps and DI modelers),
drum kit, keyboard (63 key), mics, monitors, mixer and accessories (cables and stands).

The venue provides the PA system, so amps are not needed.

We recommend you leverage the backline; however, if you have custom gear needs, call it out here:

- Pedalboard
- Amp
- Other

## Backing band support

Regardless of whether you are soloist, band, or open jam walk-in,
do you need backing band support?  If so, what instruments do you need to back you?

- Drums
- Bass
- Guitar
- Keys
- Horns (call out which ones)

Note that we share out signup details so that walk-ins can also contribute to the backing band
